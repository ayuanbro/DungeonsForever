[h2 type=bar]Tier List Considerations[/h2]

When putting together the tier list, we have assigned each specialization a rank on an overall scale from [color=q5]S[/color] to [color=q1]D[/color], which is dependent on their ranking in a number of different categories, including:

[ul]
[li][b]Offensive Throughput[/b]: Simply how much damage the spec contributes to the group.[/li]
[li][b]Buff Utility[/b]: What your spec brings to the group that benefits your fellow teammates.[/li]
[li][b]Non-Buff Utility[/b]: Less tangible utility, such as the ability to off-tank in a dire situation.[/li]
[li][b]Defensive Utility[/b]: This is a smaller consideration, but a dead DPS is a bad DPS. Having a toolkit that allows you to play safe by default will also allow you to have higher uptime on the target.[/li]
[/ul]

It's worth noting that your mileage will vary greatly from player to player and situation to situation. A clear example of this is the [color=mage]Mage[/color]'s and [color=warlock]Warlock[/color]'s ability to output by far the highest AOE damage in the game during these early levels, but if your group does not pull more than 2-3 mobs at a time, then the Mage will be greatly outshone. [color=hunter]Hunter[/color] is incredibly strong in those types of environments, but is less adept at AOE damage. 

Overall, physical DPS performs the best at level 30 outside of the two outlier classes. Casters can still keep up for the most part, but mostly through using high-value damage-over-time effects or AOE cooldowns, and then wanding for the bulk of the dungeon. [color=druid]Balance Druid[/color] is a bit of an outlier at the bottom, as they don't have any wanding option, but bring a high spell power [spell=782] on the tank to compensate.
