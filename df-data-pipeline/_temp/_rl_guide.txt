## The Ruins of Lordaeron Overview in WoW Forever

_The Ruins of Lordaeron holds remnants of the Scourge from the story of Warcraft III that still haunt the Forsaken in their home of Tirisfal Glades. Players must venture into this scourge-infested territory to push back the Lich King's army away from Lordaeron._

The recommended level for The Ruins of Lordaeron is between 16 and 22, and it has started developing the nickname of "RoL".

## Location of The Ruins of Lordaeron in WoW Forever

The Ruins of Lordaeron is easy to get into, especially for Horde players. Horde: find the portal above the Undercity, in the non-instanced Ruins of Lordaeron, where they can enter from Tirisfal Glades south of Brill.

**Horde** players can simply go to the uninstanced Ruins of Lordaeron above the Undercity to find the dungeon entrance

The Ruins of Lordaeron Death and Wipe Recovery

As with many World of Warcraft Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in The Ruins of Lordaeron can be a frustrating time.

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. The Ruins of Lordaerons Spirit Healer is located immediately outside the dungeon, so wiping here will not be as frustrating as other dungeons.

## Noteworthy Trash Mobs

### The "Spider Wing"

The intro mobs are not too serious, and these spider mobs, [毒液潜伏者](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250735/%E6%AF%92%E6%B6%B2%E6%BD%9C%E4%BC%8F%E8%80%85), [蜘蛛](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250484/%E8%9C%98%E8%9B%9B), [狼蛛](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250488/%E7%8B%BC%E8%9B%9B), are just grouped in packs and can patrol the area.

[![Image 24: Ruins of Lordaeron Trash](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303274.jpg?maxWidth=700)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303274.jpg)

**Strategy:** These packs are not too challenging in their individual status. The only issue players may have is accidentally pulling patrolling packs and overpulling. Be careful and keep an eye out as you're pulling these mobs, and pull them back.

### Skeletal Soldiers and Banshees

[骷髅士兵](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250626/%E9%AA%B7%E9%AB%85%E5%A3%AB%E5%85%B5) are physical undead soldiers that patrol the area near The Baron, along with [尖叫的女妖](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250617/%E5%B0%96%E5%8F%AB%E7%9A%84%E5%A5%B3%E5%A6%96)s near the area

[![Image 25: Ruins of Lordaeron Trash](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303271.jpg)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303271.jpg)

**Strategy:**Banshees can silence, and the soldiers can hit hard in large packs. Use stuns like [![Image 26](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_holy_sealofmight.gif)制裁之锤](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/spell=853/%E5%88%B6%E8%A3%81%E4%B9%8B%E9%94%A4) to make these packs easier.

### Flesh Golem

These [血肉傀儡](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=255109/%E8%A1%80%E8%82%89%E5%82%80%E5%84%A1)s deal intense damage and have a threat-reducing Knock Away ability.

[![Image 27: Ruins of Lordaeron Trash](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303266.jpg)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303266.jpg)

**Strategy:** Be careful when you pull them and avoid getting knocked into other packs. They patrol Lordamere Overlook, which has a healthy amount of trash around the area.

### Ghouls and Ragged Ghouls

These [血肉傀儡](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=255109/%E8%A1%80%E8%82%89%E5%82%80%E5%84%A1)s and [肮脏的食尸鬼](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250622/%E8%82%AE%E8%84%8F%E7%9A%84%E9%A3%9F%E5%B0%B8%E9%AC%BC)s deal intense damage and come in packs of 3-4. They are in close quarters with other packs as well.

[![Image 28: Ruins of Lordaeron Trash](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303259.jpg?maxWidth=400)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303259.jpg)

**Strategy:** Be careful when you pull them, as it's very easy to pull multiple packs by accident. They have powerful melee swings, so avoid overpulling, as it can be overwhelming for your tank and healer.

## Ruins of Lordaeron Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Witherfang

[![Image 29: Witherfang](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303275.jpg?maxWidth=600)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303275.jpg)

**Strategy**

[枯牙](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250483/%E6%9E%AF%E7%89%99) patrols King's Alley and is a simple tank-and-spank fight. They're accompanied by two spider mobs, and those should be taken down asap. Use stuns to make this fight easier and cleanse the **Leech Poison** debuff off of the tank when applied.

  

The Baron

[![Image 30: The Baron](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303270.jpg?maxWidth=400)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303270.jpg)

**Strategy**

[男爵](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250660/%E7%94%B7%E7%88%B5) is a hard-hitting boss with one main ability, **Knockout**. This deals high instant damage and has a threat drop in it. If your tank is not at full HP, this ability can kill the tank. Use stuns to keep the boss from casting this.

**Abilities**
[![Image 31](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_gauntlets_05.gif)击昏](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/spell=17307/%E5%87%BB%E6%98%8F)

Viktor The Vile

[![Image 32: Viktor the Vile](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303265.jpg)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303265.jpg)

**Strategy**

[[Viktor the Vile]](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=256035/viktor-the-vile) is found when players activate the fire in the chimney located in the building to the southwest in Lordamere Overlook by the Lordaeron Graveyard. Players must clear 5 waves of adds before fighting Viktor. He only has powerful melee swings and the same **Leech Poison** ability as Witherfang, so make sure you let the tank get aggro first

The Abandoned

[![Image 33: The Abandoned](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303263.jpg?maxWidth=250)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303263.jpg)

**Strategy**

[被遗弃者](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250631/%E8%A2%AB%E9%81%97%E5%BC%83%E8%80%85) will be found as soon as you enter Market Street from Lordamere Overlook. The statue in this area will trigger 3 waves of adds before The Abandoned comes out. He will **Frost Nova** players, apply a chill effect to those who physically hit him, and cast **Drain Life** on the tank.

  

Bjork

[![Image 34: Bjork](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303262.jpg?maxWidth=370)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303262.jpg)

**Strategy**

[比约克](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=256097/%E6%AF%94%E7%BA%A6%E5%85%8B) only has one ability: a very short **Anti-Magic Shield**. It goes away pretty fast, so this boss is a very quick tank and spank.

  

Rath'Mael

[![Image 35: Rathmael](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303256.jpg)](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1303256.jpg)

**Strategy**

[被遗弃者](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/cn/npc=250631/%E8%A2%AB%E9%81%97%E5%BC%83%E8%80%85) has one ability: **Flamestrike**. Move out of it as soon as he channels the cast, as it's always cast where the tank was when the cast started. You can also stun him to prevent him from casting it altogether.
