[h2 type=bar toc="Overview"]The Ruins of Lordaeron Overview in WoW Forever[/h2]
[pad]
[pad]
[box][i]The Ruins of Lordaeron holds remnants of the Scourge from the story of Warcraft III that still haunt the Forsaken in their home of Tirisfal Glades. Players must venture into this scourge-infested territory to push back the Lich King's army away from Lordaeron.[/i][/box]

The recommended level for The Ruins of Lordaeron is between 16 and 22, and it has started developing the nickname of "RoL".  

[h2 type=bar toc="Location"]Location of The Ruins of Lordaeron in WoW Forever[/h2]
[pad]
[pad]
The Ruins of Lordaeron is easy to get into, especially for Horde players. Horde: find the portal above the Undercity, in the non-instanced Ruins of Lordaeron, where they can enter from Tirisfal Glades south of Brill.

[section-header="The Ruins of Lordaeron Death and Wipe Recovery" width=600 collapse=true icon=INV_Misc_QuestionMark level=2 margin=half heading=none]
   [div]

As with many World of Warcraft Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in The Ruins of Lordaeron can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana.  Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it.  

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. The Ruins of Lordaerons Spirit Healer is located immediately outside the dungeon, so wiping here will not be as frustrating as other dungeons.

[h2 type=bar]Noteworthy Trash Mobs[/h2]

[h3 toc="Spiders"]The "Spider Wing"[/h3]
The intro mobs are not too serious, and these spider mobs, [npc=250735], [npc=250484], [npc=250488], are just grouped in packs and can patrol the area. 

[pad]
[center][table][tr][td][screenshot id=1303274 border=2 alt="Ruins of Lordaeron Trash" size=normal width=700][/screenshot][/td][/tr][/table][/center]
[pad]

[b]Strategy:[/b] These packs are not too challenging in their individual status. The only issue players may have is accidentally pulling patrolling packs and overpulling. Be careful and keep an eye out as you're pulling these mobs, and pull them back.


[h3 toc="Skeletal Mobs"] Skeletal Soldiers and Banshees[/h3]
[npc=250626] are physical undead soldiers that patrol the area near The Baron, along with [npc=250617]s near the area

[pad]
[center][table][tr][td][screenshot id=1303271 size=normal border=2 alt="Ruins of Lordaeron Trash" width=700][/screenshot][/td][td][/td][/tr][/table][/center]
[pad]

[b]Strategy: [/b]Banshees can silence, and the soldiers can hit hard in large packs. Use stuns like [spell=853] to make these packs easier.


[h3 toc="Flesh Golems"]Flesh Golem[/h3]
These [npc=255109]s deal intense damage and have a threat-reducing Knock Away ability. 

[pad]
[center][table][tr][td][screenshot id=1303266 size=normal border=2 alt="Ruins of Lordaeron Trash" width=400][/screenshot][/td]][/tr][/table][/center]
[pad]

[b]Strategy:[/b] Be careful when you pull them and avoid getting knocked into other packs. They patrol Lordamere Overlook, which has a healthy amount of trash around the area. 
[pad]


[h3 toc="Ghouls"]Ghouls and Ragged Ghouls[/h3]
These [npc=255109]s and [npc=250622]s deal intense damage and come in packs of 3-4. They are in close quarters with other packs as well.

[pad]
[center][table][tr][td][screenshot id=1303259 size=normal border=2 alt="Ruins of Lordaeron Trash" width=400][/screenshot][/td]][/tr][/table][/center]
[pad]

[b]Strategy:[/b] Be careful when you pull them, as it's very easy to pull multiple packs by accident. They have powerful melee swings, so avoid overpulling, as it can be overwhelming for your tank and healer.
