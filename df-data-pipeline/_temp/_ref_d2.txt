== PRIEST / Discipline ==
  Power Word: Fortitude | Always on.
  Inner Fire | Always on.
  Power Word: Shield | Before the pull; in groups, on the tank.
  Holy Fire | To open (before 20: Smite).
  Mind Blast | After the opener.
  Shadow Word: Pain | Damage over time.
  Smite | To open until you get Holy Fire.
  Renew | On the tank, or on you if you worry about your health.
  Heal | Your main heal in groups.
  Flash Heal | Only if someone is about to die (costs a lot of mana).
  Prayer of Healing | When the whole group is badly hurt.

== PRIEST / Shadow ==
  Power Word: Fortitude | Always on.
  Inner Fire | Always on.
  Power Word: Shield | Shield before the pull.
  Holy Fire | To open (optional; before 20: Smite).
  Mind Blast | After the opener; again if the enemy lasts.
  Shadow Word: Pain | Damage over time; in groups, on several if mana allows.
  Mind Flay | Talent: after Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Pain; watch your mana.
  Smite | To open until you get Holy Fire.
  Renew | If you worry about your health.
== SHAMAN / Elemental ==
  Strength of Earth Totem | Totems before the pull.
  Call of the Elements | Drops your totems at once (level 20).
  Lightning Bolt | To pull and as filler.
  Flame Shock | Right after; refresh it on long-lived enemies.
  Earth Shock | Alternate with Flame Shock if you don't want to hard-cast.
  Searing Totem | Last totem, right before pulling.
  Fire Nova | With 3+ enemies, on cooldown.
== SHAMAN / Enhancement ==
  Lightning Shield | Put it up between pulls.
  Lightning Bolt | To pull; then save mana and use your weapon.
  Stormstrike | On cooldown (talent); then consume its effect with Earth Shock.
  Earth Shock | To pull or finish; with Stormstrike, on cooldown.
  Flame Shock | To pull or finish; in a group, alternate it with Earth Shock.
  Searing Totem | With several enemies or in dungeons.
  Fire Nova | Several enemies: on cooldown, with a fire totem down and while mana lasts.
== SHAMAN / Restoration ==
  Strength of Earth Totem | Support totems for your group.
  Call of the Elements | If you drop 2+ totems at once.
  Healing Wave | On anyone badly hurt, above all the tank.
  Searing Totem | Several enemies, if damage taken is low.
  Fire Nova | Several enemies, while mana lasts.
== MAGE / Arcane ==
  Arcane Intellect | Always on.
  Frost Armor | Armor always on.
  Frostbolt | To pull: the enemy arrives slowed.
  Arcane Blast | Up to 2-4 stacks (more stacks, more mana), then Arcane Missiles.
  Arcane Missiles | After Arcane Blast, or when Missile Barrage lights up.
  Fire Blast | While moving or to finish.
  Frost Nova | To get away from several enemies.
  Flamestrike | Several enemies together.
  Cone of Cold | To slow several enemies.
  Arcane Explosion | Several enemies close.
== MAGE / Fire ==
  Arcane Intellect | Always on.
  Frost Armor | Armor always on.
  Pyroblast | To open.
  Fireball | Filler.
  Fire Blast | While moving or to finish.
  Frost Nova | If it gets close: freeze and back off.
  Flamestrike | Several enemies: cast it first.
  Blast Wave | Level 30 talent: several enemies after Flamestrike, or when you need to slow them.
  Arcane Explosion | Several enemies close.
== MAGE / Frost ==
  Arcane Intellect | Always on.
  Frost Armor | Armor always on.
  Frostbolt | Your main spell until the enemy is almost dead.
  Frost Nova | Use it often when hit: freeze and back off.
  Ice Lance | While the enemy is frozen or when Fingers of Frost procs (talent, level 30).
  Fire Blast | To finish.
  Blizzard | 3+ enemies.
  Arcane Explosion | 3+ enemies if they won't last a full Blizzard or you must move.
== WARLOCK / Affliction ==
  Summon Succubus | Send your demon in first (before 20: Imp or Voidwalker).
  Bane of Agony | First damage over time: low threat.
  Corruption | After Bane of Agony.
  Immolate | After Corruption.
  Siphon Life | If the enemy will last 20 seconds or more.
  Shadow Bolt | When it turns instant (Nightfall).
  Drain Life | When you need health.
  Drain Soul | To finish if you need shards.
  Life Tap | Trade health for mana when your health is safe.
== WARLOCK / Demonology ==

  Bane of Agony | First damage over time: low threat.
  Corruption | After Bane of Agony.
  Immolate | After Corruption; then, wand.
  Life Tap | Trade health for mana when your health is safe.
== WARLOCK / Destruction ==
  Summon Succubus | Send your demon in first (before 20: Imp or Voidwalker).
  Bane of Agony | Damage over time.
  Corruption | Damage over time.
  Immolate | Your main damage over time.
  Shadow Bolt | Filler.
  Conflagrate | Near the end of Immolate or to finish (consumes Immolate).
  Shadowburn | To finish (you get the shard back).
  Searing Pain | When you need a faster cast (high threat).
  Drain Soul | To finish if you need shards.
  Life Tap | Trade health for mana when your health is safe.
== DRUID / Balance ==
  Wrath | To open from max range and as filler until it dies.
  Moonfire | After the opener: keep it on every enemy.
  Insect Swarm | After Moonfire (talent, level 25).
  Entangling Roots | To keep it away from you; recast if it breaks free.
  Rejuvenation | On yourself when needed or between pulls.
== DRUID / Feral Combat (Bear) ==
  Wrath | To pull from range, before entering Bear Form.
  Bear Form | Fight in Bear Form (class quest at level 10).
  Demoralizing Roar | Keep it on the enemies you tank; since the Oct 8 patch it also puts some threat on every target hit.
  Enrage | At the start: gives you Rage (lowers your armor a bit).
  Primal Bite | Your main Rage spender; use it on Clearcasting.
  Maul | Single target, to spend extra Rage.
  Swipe | Several enemies.
  Moonfire | Before level 10: keep it up and cast Wrath while it comes.
== DRUID / Feral Combat (Cat) ==
  Cat Form | Fight in Cat Form (class quest at level 20).
  Rip | At 4-5 points if the enemy will last 6+ seconds.
  Shifting Power | On cooldown, with less than 60 Energy (it can't be used at full Energy).
  Shred | Your main builder when you are behind the enemy; use it on Clearcasting.
  Claw | Your main builder when you can't get behind the enemy.
  Bear Form | Before 20: fight in Bear Form.
  Maul | Before 20, in Bear Form: your main attack.
== DRUID / Restoration ==
  Thorns | On the tank: helps with threat.
  Rejuvenation | On the tank while fighting.
  Regrowth | When the tank needs a direct heal.
  Healing Touch | If Regrowth is already on.
  Entangling Roots | If the pull gets dangerous: root one enemy.
  Moonfire | With spare mana: help with damage.
  Wrath | With spare mana, after Moonfire.
