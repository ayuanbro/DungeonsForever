Library Books
Library Books are collectible books found throughout the world in WoW Forever. Originally introduced in Season of Discovery as part of the Mage Rune system, Library Books can now be collected and turned in by characters of any class to complete the Friend of the Library quest. In this WoW Forever Library Books guide, you’ll learn where to find all Library Books, where to turn them in, and what rewards you can unlock by completing the Friend of the Library quest.

Where to Turn In Library Books in WoW Forever
You can turn in Library Books individually at your faction's librarian. Each book is a separate quest:
Alliance:
Garion Wendell
in the Mage Quarter of
Stormwind City
.
Horde:
Owen Thadd
in the Magic Quarter of
Undercity
.
You need to carry a Library Book for the corresponding turn-in quest to appear. Each different book you turn in counts towards
Friend of the Library
and
Greater Friend of the Library
.

Update October 2, 2026: New Library Books Quest
The latest beta patch increased the level cap to 30, and a third Library Books quest became available. You unlock the quest after turning in 25 Library Books.

Library Book turn-ins are cumulative across all Friend of the Library quests. After turning in 10 books for the first quest, you only need 10 more to reach 20 for
