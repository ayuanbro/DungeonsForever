Changing Tastes

Enter the Excavation Sites in the Wetlands and bring back Thicket Raptor Meat.
Thicket Raptor Meat (4)

Description

Zamja tells me we need more homestyle cooking. If we start selling raptor, she thinks it'll draw in customers who don't usually visit Borstan's Firepit. Her idea is not so bad. But I want something that rises above the smells of our birth villages. We're in Orgrimmar! Borstan should serve a grand take on their familiar favorites. A hunter recently came back from the Wetlands with a giant talon from the thicket raptors in the Excavation Sites. Up for a job?
