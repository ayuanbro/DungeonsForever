WoW Forever Library Books

Library Books are collectible books found throughout the world in WoW Forever. Originally introduced in Season of Discovery as part of the Mage Rune system, Library Books can now be collected and turned in by characters of any class to complete the Friend of the Library quest. In this WoW Forever Library Books guide, you’ll learn where to find all Library Books, where to turn them in, and what rewards you can unlock by completing the Friend of the Library quest.

Where to Turn In Library Books in WoW Forever

You can turn in Library Books individually at your faction’s librarian. Each book is a separate quest:

  • Alliance: Garion Wendell in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind City.
  • Horde: Owen Thadd in the Magic Quarter of Undercity.

You need to carry a Library Book for the corresponding turn-in quest to appear. Each different book you turn in counts towards Friend of the Library and Greater Friend of the Library.

WoW Forever Friend of the Library Rewards

Update October 2, 2026: New Library Books Quest
The latest beta patch increased the level cap to 30, and a third Library Books quest became available. You unlock the quest after turning in 25 Library Books.

Turning in Library Books unlocks three reward quests in WoW Forever:

Library Book turn-ins are cumulative across all Friend of the Library quests. After turning in 10 books for the first quest, you only need 10 more to reach 20 for Greater Friend of the Library, and then 5 more to reach 25 for Greater Friend of the Library. In total, you only need to turn in 25 Library Books to earn all three quest rewards.

Mage Research from Library Books

Mages can also unlock the new  Research profession by turning in Library Books. Current Beta reports indicate that  Research becomes available after the first Library Book turn-in.

 Research allows Mages to use  Study while inside a suitable library:

  • Requires 1x  Light Feather.
  • Has a 1-hour cooldown.
  • Creates  Bundle of Scrolls.
  • The bundle contains several Mage scrolls, e.g.  Scroll of Imbue Lesser Flame or  Scroll of Imbue Chillknife.

Research is currently affected by beta bugs and may temporarily disappear after logging out.
