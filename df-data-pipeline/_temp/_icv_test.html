Enchanting in World of Warcraft Forever is a primary crafting profession that breaks down uncommon and rare gear into magical essences and dust, which are then used to permanently augment armor and weapons with powerful stat bonuses or create consumable utility items.

1.

Enchanting Profession Overview in WoW Forever

Enchanting is a primary crafting profession that uses magical materials to permanently improve weapons, armor, and other equipment. This overview covers what Enchanting does in WoW Forever, where trainers can be found for each rank, the best professions to pair with it, and the benefits and campfire features it provides.

2.

What Is Enchanting?

Enchanters use dust, essences, shards, and other magical materials to add permanent bonuses to equipment. These enchants typically improve stats, defenses, or damage, making the profession useful to every class. Enchanters obtain many of their materials through Disenchant, which destroys unwanted uncommon or better weapons and armor and turns them into enchanting materials.

Enchanting also lets you craft wands, staves, off-hands, and class specific relics; enchanting is one of the few ways for players to get these caster-based items such as wands outside of a random quest or dungeon drop. Enchanting can also make oils, a consumable item that provides a temporary stat boost for casters by coating their weapon in oil.

Many enchants require an appropriate runed enchanting rod in your inventory, starting with a Runed Copper Rod. As your Enchanting skill increases, you will need stronger rods for higher-level recipes. These rods can be expensive to make, but each upgrade marks a milestone in your progression as an Enchanter, giving you access to more advanced enchants and crafts.

3.

Best Profession to Pair with Enchanting

Tailoring is the usual second profession for an Enchanter, as Tailoring can turn cloth into uncommon or better equipment that you can then Disenchant for materials. Because cloth used by Tailoring drops directly from enemies, you can gather it without needing a separate gathering profession. You can also disenchant unwanted gear from quests and dungeons, or buy enchanting materials from other players.

4.

Enchanting Campfire Items and Buffs

Enchanters can make items to place at a campsite once they have the required Enchanting skill; each item requires a nearby campfire. The skill required to place each item is shown below:

Campfire Item Skill to Place What It Does
Enchanted Lute 20 Grants increased Armor, stats, and resistances to players sitting nearby; the buff does not stack with Mark of the Wild.
Arcane Salvager 140 Allows more efficient disenchanting at the camp and provides the Enchanted Lute benefit.
Arcane Forge 300 Places an Arcane Forge for recipes that require one, and provides the Enchanted Lute benefit.
5.

Enchanting Trainers in WoW Forever

Enchanting has four trainable ranks, each of which increases the highest skill level that you can reach. You can learn the next rank before reaching your current skill cap, provided you meet both requirements shown below.

Rank Skill Cap Enchanting Skill Required Character Level Required
Apprentice 75 None None
Journeyman 150 50 10
Expert 225 125 20
Artisan 300 200 35
