== WARRIOR / Arms ==
  Battle Shout | Before the pull, and keep it up all fight.
  Bloodrage | Free rage: best between pulls.
  Charge | To open (out of combat only).
  Rend | Bleed: keep it up if the enemy will last.
  Slam | Right after your auto-attack (if a swing is about to land, skip to the next one).
  Thunder Clap | Keep it up: the enemy attacks slower.
  Victory Rush | After a kill: free hit that heals you.
  Overpower | As soon as it lights up (the enemy dodged).
  Demoralizing Shout | Against melee enemies: they hit softer.
  Sunder Armor | Once or twice early (not below half health).
  Heroic Strike | Only when you have rage to spare.
  Cleave | 2 or more enemies together.
  Sweeping Strikes | With 2 enemies together and rage saved up.
== WARRIOR / Fury ==
  Battle Shout | Before the pull, and keep it up.
  Bloodrage | Free rage: best between pulls.
  Charge | To open (out of combat only).
  Rend | If the enemy will live through the whole bleed.
  Overpower | As soon as it lights up (the enemy dodged).
  Victory Rush | After a kill: free hit that heals you.
  Sunder Armor | If the enemy is above 70% health.
  Heroic Strike | To spend spare rage.
  Cleave | Instead of Heroic Strike with 2+ enemies.
  Demoralizing Shout | Against melee enemies.
== WARRIOR / Protection ==
  Defensive Stance | Tanking stance (class quest at level 10).
  Charge | To open: from Battle Stance (until Vanguard at level 25).
  Bloodrage | At the start: free rage.
  Shield Block | Use it a lot: less damage and it lights up Revenge.
  Revenge | As soon as it lights up.
  Thunder Clap | Several enemies: threat and slower attacks.
  Demoralizing Shout | So they hit you softer (melee enemies); since the Oct 8 patch it also puts some threat on every target hit.
  Sunder Armor | In groups and on bosses: 5 stacks (low threat on the beta).
  Rend | If you already have solid threat.
  Heroic Strike | With spare rage.
  Shield Bash | Interrupt casts.
  Battle Shout | If you are the only Warrior in the group.
== PALADIN / Holy ==
  Blessing of Might | On you and your group.
  Devotion Aura | Aura always on (or Retribution Aura).
  Seal of Righteousness | To deal damage: keep a seal up.
  Judgement | On cooldown.
  Holy Strike | On cooldown.
  Consecration | Big damage, but costs a lot of mana.
  Flash of Light | Your main heal from level 20.
  Holy Light | Big, expensive heal: only with spare mana or someone in danger.
  Holy Shock | Instant heal for emergencies.
  Lay on Hands | Last resort: big heal that uses all your mana.
== PALADIN / Protection ==
  Righteous Fury | Always on: more threat.
  Retribution Aura | Aura always on (before 16: Devotion Aura).
  Seal of Fury | Put it on before the pull and keep it up.
  Judgement | On cooldown.
  Exorcism | Against undead and demons.
  Holy Strike | On cooldown.
  Consecration | With several enemies, first; with one, if mana allows.
== PALADIN / Retribution ==
  Blessing of Might | Always on: more attack power.
  Seal of Righteousness | Your seal: put it on and renew it when it ends. Best with a fast weapon or lots of spell power.
  Seal of Command | Instead of Seal of Righteousness only with a slow weapon without on-hit effects.
  Judgement | On cooldown.
  Holy Strike | On cooldown.
  Exorcism | On cooldown against undead and demons.
  Consecration | Only with several enemies (costs a lot of mana).
  Hammer of Justice | Stun a dangerous enemy.

== ROGUE / Assassination ==
  Stealth | Sneak up unseen.
  Ambush | Opener from Stealth from behind (dagger).
  Cheap Shot | Another opener: stun and an early edge.
  Mutilate | Your main builder once you have it (talent).
  Backstab | Main builder until Mutilate, if you are behind.
  Sinister Strike | If you can't get behind.
  Slice and Dice | At 5 points if the enemy will last.
  Eviscerate | At 5 points if Slice and Dice is already up.
  Expose Armor | In groups with heavy physical damage and long fights.
  Kick | Interrupt casts.
  Gouge | Incapacitate the enemy to take less damage.

== ROGUE / Subtlety ==
  Stealth | Sneak up unseen.
  Ambush | Opener from Stealth from behind (dagger).
  Cheap Shot | Another opener: stun and an early edge.
  Backstab | Your main builder: dagger, from behind.
  Hemorrhage | From level 30 (talent): your main builder at all times; no need to be behind.
  Sinister Strike | If you can't get behind.
  Slice and Dice | Keep it up at all times; refresh it right as it ends.
  Rupture | From level 30 with this build: on enemies that live its full duration; better than Slice and Dice.
  Eviscerate | At 5 points if Slice and Dice is already up.
  Expose Armor | In groups with heavy physical damage and long fights.
  Kick | Interrupt casts.
  Gouge | Incapacitate the enemy to take less damage.
