[h2 type=bar]Tier List Considerations[/h2]
[pad]
[pad]
When putting together the tier list, we have assigned each specialization a rank on an overall scale from [color=q5]S[/color] to [color=q1]D[/color], which is dependent on their ranking in a number of different categories, including:

[ul]
[li][b]Survivability[/b]: This includes the specialization's ability to survive high damage as a Main Tank, such as damage-reduction abilities, cooldowns, overall durability, and potential self-sustain.[/li]
[li][b]Damage & Threat[/b]: This takes into account how much damage a specialization can deal and the amount of threat it can generate while still focusing on staying alive and main-tanking.[/li]
[li][b]Trash & Adds[/b]: This focuses mainly on AoE threat generation and survivability.[/li]
[li][b]Group Utility[/b]: This encompasses the utility a specialization has that helps the party overall.[/li]
[/ul]

[b]Note[/b]: Each tank will have aspects that they are better or worse at dealing with than other tanks. A high ranking does not automatically mean a tank will be particularly adept in all aspects of the game. But at level 30, the value of AOE threat and damage is a lot higher than it might be at the end-game, which skews the tier list.

Honorary mention: [color=shaman]Enhancement Shamans[/color] do have tanking support, but don't have a full-fledged tank toolkit. They will be perfectly fine to bring as a dungeon tank, but bringing a Shaman tank to more difficult content will be ill-advised. 

[h2 type=bar toc="Tier List Breakdown"]WOW: Forever Tank Tier List Breakdown and Explanations[/h2]

As we mentioned above, a number of points were considered when assigning each specialization its overall rank, but the actual usage of certain tanks will differ depending on what type of content you're engaging in and what your party or raid might need.

[b]With that being said, you can clear all content in WoW: Forever with any tank specialization. Each tank will have their own niche, and if a particular tank becomes unviable, they will likely receive changes to bring them closer to the other tanks.[/b]

[b]Rankings:[/b]
[ol]
[li]Protection Paladin (S-Tier)[/li]
[li]Protection Warrior (A-Tier)[/li]
[li]Feral Druid (A-Tier)[/li]
[/ol]

[color=paladin]Protection Paladin[/color] stands out as a pick at level 30 as they get access to rank 2 of [spell=26573]. With [spell=7294] scaling with spell power and mana concerns being all but eliminated as the Paladin gets access to more talent points like [spell=20150], they never struggle with threat and most often stake their spot at the top of the damage meters. Their new level 30 cooldown [spell=1311015] also has a lot higher value in the early levels compared to the other tanks. 

[color=warrior]Protection Warriors[/color] and [color=druid]Feral Druids[/color] perform perfectly fine as well, however. Warriors have increased threat modifiers through spells like [spell=6572] and can now use [spell=6343] in [spell=71]. Because [spell=2565] is rage neutral with a rather low cooldown, they can feel sturdier as the other tanks haven't quite got the equivalent tools yet.

Druids can bring their own [spell=467], which deals a lot of damage at level 30, and have access to talents that increase the damage of [spell=779]. While Swipe doesn't scale with Attack Power yet, it is confirmed to be coming in a future build of the beta. Even without attack power modifiers, it can still deal solid damage through talents like [spell=16947] and [spell=16998]. Feral Druids also have access to [spell=407995] and [spell=17007] at level 30, which is enormously valuable.
