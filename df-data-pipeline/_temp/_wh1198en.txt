# In Search of Thaelrid

Seek out [Argent Guard Thaelrid](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4787/argent-guard-thaelrid) in [Blackfathom Deeps](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/zone=719/blackfathom-deeps).

## Relevant Locations

Part of this quest happens in [Darnassus](javascript:) and [Blackfathom Deeps](javascript:).

Tip: Click map to zoom

[](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4786)

Hide pins

[Darnassus](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/zone=1657/darnassus)

## Description

Many rumors circulate these days about the union of the Twilight's Hammer and the naga in [Blackfathom Deeps](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/zone=719/blackfathom-deeps) on the coasts of Ashenvale.  
  
My role here with the Argent Dawn is to collect as much information as I can, so we know when and where help is needed.  
  
It has been weeks now since I sent a scout to check on the activity at [Blackfathom Deeps](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/zone=719/blackfathom-deeps). [Argent Guard Thaelrid](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4787/argent-guard-thaelrid) has not yet reported back.  
  
My other scouts are preoccupied with pressing matters.  
  
Seek out and aid Thaelrid for me, <name>.
## [Completion](javascript:)

[Dawnwatcher Shaedlass](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4786/dawnwatcher-shaedlass) was good to send you. There is indeed a wicked plot underway in this corrupted place.

## Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain: 
*   5,700 experience 
*   150 reputation with [Argent Dawn](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/faction=529/argent-dawn) 
*   150 reputation with [Darnassus](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/faction=69/darnassus) 

Level 34 Min: 24 34
