Item stats in the Forever client come from StatPercentEditor × RandPropPoints (item level, quality, slot); armor from ItemArmorTotal, ItemArmorQuality and ArmorLocation; weapon damage from the ItemDamage tables and the item's speed. Of the items both clients share, 97.6% of the stats, 92% of the armor totals and 91% of the damage ranges computed this way equal Classic's stored values; the rest are the items Forever retuned.

Names, icons and item data © Blizzard Entertainment. Read from the client's own tables through wago.tools. Some items are not in those tables: Blizzard's server sends them to the game the first time it meets one, and we read the ones our game has received from its cache. No build-to-build history yet: each item is compared with Classic Era only.
