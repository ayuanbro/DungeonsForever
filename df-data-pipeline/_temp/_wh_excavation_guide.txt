Excavation Site Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever
The Explorer's League, a band of archeologists researching the origins of the Dwarven race, has long had a presence in the mountainous regions of the Wetlands with Whelgar's Excavation Site, a camp overrun with raptors, serving as its main hub. Strange activity was noted to the southeast of the site, and a new camp was set up to explore the newly uncovered titan activity.
The recommended level for Excavation Site is between 24 and 30.
Location of Excavation Site in World of Warcraft: Forever
Excavation Site is located in
Wetlands
and is a new installment in WoW: Forever. In previous Classic iterations, this area was inaccessible. But with newly constructed roads, you can now find the entrance to the new dungeon at the top of the hill. The
Entrance to Excavation Site
Meeting Stone and Entry
Alliance
can easily access the dungeon from Menethil Harbor, which now has a boat connection with Stormwind through Auberdine, or you can run on foot through Loch Modan. Most players will have started their questing journey in the Wetlands at this point and will likely have a flight path in the zone already.
Horde
will have a slightly longer trek, as they have to go through Hillsbrad Foothills and Arathi Highlands on foot to reach Wetlands. Make sure to pick up flight paths in Tarren Mill and Hammerfall during your walk to make your future experience slightly more pleasant.
As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Excavation Site can be a frustrating time due to the long run-back. Any player who has made the infamous run from Teldrassil to Ironforge will know this graveyard very well, as it's the only one in the zone. If your group fully wipes, you'll have to run across half the Wetlands map before reentering the dungeon.
Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it.
If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.
